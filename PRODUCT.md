# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams are the first audience for the homepage. Peers and collaborators may also visit, but are not the primary audience for its first impression.

## Product Purpose

zeriax.com is a personal portfolio: show what zeriax has designed and built, make the work easy to inspect, and provide a way to get in touch. The user wants the homepage to present them as a student working across product design and software engineering, rather than leading with just one of those roles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the public name **zeriax**.
- Preserve the existing portfolio, blog, socials, theme control, and project links while redesigning the homepage.
- Interactive blog posts are a future goal, not a current feature to claim as shipped.
- The user does not want to pay for a commercial web-font licence. Do not ship Klim fonts or test fonts without the appropriate licence.
- The user rejected a standalone Z logo, faux-technical numbering, generic slogans, and a blue grid as identity devices.

## Evidence on Hand

- The homepage lists current DollarDeploy experience and concrete technologies.
- The portfolio lists DollarDeploy, ruoka-rs, and maykbot-ts, with links to the work.
- The blog currently has one sample MDX post. There are no finished interactive essays or case-study assets in the repository to present as finished work.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with inspectable work rather than claims about taste or process.
2. Keep the author's voice recognisable; do not replace it with agency copy.
3. Let design and engineering both be visible without inventing a role title or achievements.
